📦 Mallow Trading Company

Core Keys & Principles to Commerce in Another World

Key #1
Leverage Modern Quality & Variety
Introduce reliable, high-quality goods (spices, tools, household items) sourced via online catalog access that far exceed the crafting standards of the local medieval market.
Key #2
Fair & Transparent Pricing
Undercut greedy merchant monopolies while maintaining reasonable profit margins. Earning customer trust builds long-term loyalty over short-term greed.
Key #3
Discreet Supply Chain Management
Protect the secret origin of stock (the catalog/online purchasing ability) to prevent political interference, guild target creation, or power imbalances.
Key #4
Build Strong Local Partnerships
Collaborate with reliable local adventurers, guilds, and nobility to secure trade routes, handle legal protections, and foster community goodwill.
Key #5
Adapt Product Offerings to Local Needs
Focus on practical everyday solutions—such as hygiene products, ready-to-eat meals, and sturdy gear—that solve immediate, real-world problems for locals.
